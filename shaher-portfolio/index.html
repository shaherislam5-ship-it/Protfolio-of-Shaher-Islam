Questions clients ask first

What do I need to get started?

A clear idea of what you sell, who buys it and the result you want, whether that is sales, leads or messages. A Facebook Page and ad account help. If you don’t have them yet, we can set them up together.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your product, market and goal. We can start with a modest test budget, see what works, then put more behind what performs. I’ll suggest a starting budget once we’ve talked.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest advertiser can guarantee exact sales or ROAS, because your offer, price and creative all matter. What I do commit to is a clear plan, a clean setup, regular testing and honest reporting.

Who pays for the ad spend?

Ad spend goes straight to Meta from your own ad account, and my service fee is separate. That way you always stay in control of your budget.

How soon will I see results?

New campaigns need a short learning period before performance settles, so the first days are about testing. I’ll share what I’m seeing and what I’m changing along the way.

Can you also create the ad creatives?

Yes. Content creation is one of my services, from promo concepts to ad-ready visuals and copy in Bangla or English.

Do you work with clients outside Bangladesh?

Yes. I work with businesses in Bangladesh and can plan campaigns for other markets too. Message me your target country and we’ll take it from there.
