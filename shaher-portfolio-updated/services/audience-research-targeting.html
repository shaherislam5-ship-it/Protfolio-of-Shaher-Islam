Focused service delivery built around your business goal, audience and available campaign data.

What it includes

A practical approach to audience research & targeting

Targeting is about understanding who the offer is for and translating that understanding into practical campaign audiences. I research audience signals before building the targeting approach.

  • Customer and offer analysis
  • Audience persona and signal research
  • Interest and behavior exploration
  • Audience segmentation
  • Targeting tests and refinement
How I approach it

Clear, measurable, and adaptable

I start with the business objective, build the relevant campaign or content workflow, then use available performance signals to decide what should be refined next.
